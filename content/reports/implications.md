## Why overhaul math communication?
- Make sure AI is integrated into mathematics research effectively and responsibly
- Keep human work and understanding valued, rather than letting AI output dominate the field

## Implications
- **For researchers:** research becomes easier to share and understand
- **For the public:** clearer signals about what is known, and how it came to be known
- **For funding:** what gets funded shapes what gets done. If efficiency is valued above all else, funding may shift to AI-driven research at a cost to human understanding.
