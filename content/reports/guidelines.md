## Every submission must

1. **List the paper's main Lean results**, with a link to the Lean development, plus any cited Lean result whose proof no one has declared they understand. Citing another paper on this site links its declarations automatically.
2. **Name who understands what.** For each result, list the people who declare they understand its specification and its proof. Declarations are signed and public, so if a result turns out to be wrong or mis-specified, it can be traced to the people who vouched for it.

## Declaring understanding

- **Specification:** only if you have read the formal statement and confirmed it says what the paper claims.
- **Proof:** only if you could explain why it works without appealing to "Lean accepted it."
- Declarations can be withdrawn in a later version, and the paper's level is recomputed.
